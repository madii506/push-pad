How it works

01

Pick a person

Any handle on X. We check the account exists and pull their real name, picture and follower count before anything is signed.

02

Set the slider

10% to 100% of the creator fee pledged to ads. The rest stays with you. The number is printed on the coin's page and cannot be edited later.

03

The ads run

The pledged share buys ads on the platforms you picked. Each buy is posted in the journal with the platform, the amount, the date and a link to the live post.

04

They get paid

Their share waits in escrow from the first trade. They claim it by posting the code below — no wallet, no password, no permission asked.

Straight answers

FAQ

Do I need the person's permission?

No. Anyone can launch a coin on any public handle, and the person can ignore it forever. What they cannot lose is their share — it accrues from the first trade whether or not they ever show up. Nothing here is endorsed by the people it is launched for.

How does someone claim their share?

They post the claim code shown in the launch form from the account the coin is named after. The code is a deterministic hash of the handle, so it can be recomputed by anyone and cannot be guessed for someone else. No wallet is needed to start.

How do I know an ad was really bought?

You do not take our word for it. The ad wallet address is public, so its balance is readable on the explorer, and every buy is listed in the journal with the amount and a link to the live post. Two columns — money in, money out — and you compare them yourself.

Where do the numbers on this page come from?

The handle data is read live from X. The volume, price and market cap in the calculator are read live from Dexscreener for the contract you paste. The split percentages are arithmetic on the Pons fee, done in your browser. There are no numbers on this page that we typed in by hand.

What happens if nobody buys?

Then there is no fee, no ad budget and no ads, and the journal stays empty — which is exactly what it shows right now. A pad that invents activity before it has any is lying to you.
