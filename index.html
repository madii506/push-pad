Straight answers

FAQ

Do I need the person's permission?

No. Anyone can launch a coin on any public handle, and the person can ignore it forever. What they cannot lose is their share — it accrues from the first trade whether or not they ever show up. Nothing here is endorsed by the people it is launched for.

How does someone claim their share?

They post the claim code shown in the launch form from the account the coin is named after. The code is a deterministic hash of the handle, so anyone can recompute it and nobody can guess it for someone else. No wallet is needed to start.

How do I know an ad was really bought?

You do not take our word for it. The ad wallet address is public, so its balance is readable on the explorer, and every buy is listed with the amount and a link to the live post. Money in, money out, and you compare them yourself.

Where do the numbers on this page come from?

Handle data is read live from X. Volume, price, liquidity and market cap in the calculator are read live from Dexscreener for the contract you paste. The split percentages are arithmetic on the Pons fee, done in your browser. There are no numbers here that we typed in by hand.

What happens if nobody buys?

Then there is no fee, no ad budget and no ads, and the journal stays empty — which is exactly what it shows right now. A pad that invents activity before it has any is lying to you.
